Reach4.0

Lenovo’s REACHit app lets you connect and search multiple file storage locations in one place. Lenovo worked with Microsoft to integrate their app with Cortana so that users can use Cortana to find any content in any storage location that is connected to REACHit. Cortana and REACHit works on any PC or Tablet running Windows 10, no matter what brand it is. Users can also use natural language like ‘find the photos I took at Jane’s wedding’, and REACHit will use metadata like file-type, keywords, dates and locations to produce the most relevant results.

You can search for files and emails stored on:

Any device running Windows 10
Cloud accounts: Google Drive, Dropbox, Box, OneDrive
Outlook.com, Microsoft Exchange, Gmail, IMAP
And you can also search for files you may have looked at or created during meetings on your Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar, or Exchange Calendar.
